What this is
A structured route into process and manufacturing engineering, built on published work rather than course certificates.
A 4-month, 16-week program for anyone moving into a manufacturing, process, or applications engineering role: new graduates, technicians stepping up, engineers changing industries. It's built on Lean Six Sigma fundamentals, DMAIC, root cause, SPC, process capability, paired with the AI skills that make an engineer faster without replacing their judgment: building dashboards, automating repetitive reporting, and creating templates that make sure work is complete and correct before it goes out. Four phases, one per month, each ending in a real deliverable. Eight tracks hold the curriculum, the portfolio projects with their reports and publishing checklists, the job pipeline and STAR bank, and a week-by-week plan.
